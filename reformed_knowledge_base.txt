
KNOWLEDGE BASE — REFORMED EVANGELICAL THEOLOGY (EXCERPT)

1. THE AUTHORITY OF SCRIPTURE  
Reformed Evangelical Christianity begins with the conviction that Scripture alone (Sola Scriptura) is the ultimate authority for faith and life. The Bible is inspired, infallible, and sufficient. It reveals God’s character, humanity’s need, and the plan of redemption centered in Jesus Christ. Scripture interprets Scripture, and all doctrines must be grounded in the text.

2. THE NATURE OF GOD  
God is eternal, unchanging, sovereign, holy, just, and merciful. He exists as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—three persons, one God. His works in creation, providence, salvation, and judgment display His glory. God’s sovereignty means He governs all things to the end He has appointed.

3. HUMANITY AND SIN  
Humans are made in God’s image with dignity and purpose. Yet the Fall has corrupted every aspect of mankind (total depravity). Humans cannot save themselves and depend entirely on God’s grace. Sin separates people from God, distorts desires, blinds understanding, and enslaves the will.

4. CHRIST AND REDEMPTION  
Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, the only mediator between God and humanity. His life fulfilled righteousness; His death satisfied divine justice; His resurrection secured victory over sin and death. Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone.

5. THE HOLY SPIRIT  
The Spirit convicts of sin, regenerates the heart, grants faith, unites believers to Christ, sanctifies them, and preserves them. The Spirit illuminates Scripture and empowers believers for godly living and witness.

6. THE CHURCH  
The church is the people of God, called, redeemed, and gathered by Christ. It exists to preach the Word, administer the sacraments, practice church discipline, and make disciples. The church is one, holy, catholic (universal), and apostolic.

7. THE ORDO SALUTIS (ORDER OF SALVATION)  
Reformed theology affirms an ordered understanding of salvation: effectual calling, regeneration, faith, repentance, justification, adoption, sanctification, perseverance, and glorification. All of this is rooted in God’s eternal decree.

8. THE CHRISTIAN LIFE  
Believers are called to holiness, repentance, obedience, prayer, fellowship, and love. Good works flow from salvation but never earn it. The Christian life is lived before the face of God (coram Deo) and depends on the means of grace.

9. THE LAST THINGS  
Christ will return bodily and visibly to judge the living and the dead. The redeemed will dwell with God eternally; the unrepentant will face eternal separation. Heaven and the new creation will be the fullness of God’s restoration.

END OF EXCERPT.
